# Arrival

## Set and setting

- The harbor, day 1, dusk. Late autumn. The ferry is an hour late.
- Mara, 34, back for the first time in fifteen years. Tomas, her uncle, sixties.
- Diesel, wet rope, the ice plant that used to be there is gone.

## Beats

- Mara steps off the ferry. The ticket office is already shut.
- She sees Tomas before he sees her. He's older than she let him be in her head.
- He turns.
- "You look like your mother."
- They walk up to the customs shed without saying anything that matters. She keeps her hands in her pockets.
- At the shed he has the key out before they reach the door.
